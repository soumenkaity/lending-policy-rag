# Loan Products

## Personal Loan

Unsecured loan for salaried and self-employed individuals. Loan amount ranges from Rs 50,000 to Rs 15,00,000. Tenure ranges from 12 to 60 months. Interest rates range from 11.5% to 24% per annum on a reducing-balance basis, based on the applicant's risk grade.

## Business Term Loan

Unsecured loan for registered businesses for working capital or expansion. Loan amount ranges from Rs 5,00,000 to Rs 50,00,000. Tenure ranges from 12 to 48 months. Interest rates range from 14% to 26% per annum.

## Supplier Invoice Financing (Markup Loan)

Short-term financing in which Northwind Credit pays the merchant's approved supplier directly and the merchant repays the amount plus a fixed markup. Financing covers up to 80% of the invoice value. Tenure ranges from 30 to 120 days. The markup ranges from 1.5% to 3% per month, fixed at the time of approval and charged on the financed amount. The supplier must be onboarded and verified before the first financing request.

## Risk Grades

Every approved application is assigned a risk grade from A (lowest risk) to E (highest risk). The risk grade determines the interest rate or markup within the product range. Grade E applicants are capped at 50% of the maximum loan amount for the product.
