# Collections and Hardship Policy

## Delinquency Buckets

Overdue accounts are classified by days past due (DPD): SMA-0 for 1 to 30 DPD, SMA-1 for 31 to 60 DPD, and SMA-2 for 61 to 90 DPD. An account overdue for more than 90 days is classified as a non-performing asset (NPA).

## Fair Collection Practices

Collection agents must not contact borrowers before 8:00 AM or after 7:00 PM. Agents must carry an authorisation letter and identity card, and must never use threatening or abusive language. All collection calls are recorded.

## Financial Hardship Support

Borrowers facing genuine financial hardship, such as job loss or medical emergency, may request a moratorium of up to 3 months. Interest continues to accrue during the moratorium and is added to the outstanding principal. A borrower may receive at most one moratorium during the loan tenure.

## Restructuring

Loans may be restructured once, by extending the tenure by up to 12 months, if the borrower's account is not yet classified as an NPA.

## Grievance Redressal

Complaints should first be raised with customer support, which responds within 7 days. Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the Grievance Redressal Officer, who must resolve them within 30 days. If the complaint is still unresolved, the borrower may approach the RBI Ombudsman.
