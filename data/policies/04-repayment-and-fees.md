# Repayment and Fees Policy

## EMI Dates

Borrowers may choose the 5th, 10th or 15th of each month as their EMI date. EMIs are collected through an auto-debit (NACH or UPI AutoPay) mandate registered before disbursement.

## Processing Fee

A processing fee of 2% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of Rs 1,999, is deducted from the disbursed amount. GST at 18% applies to the processing fee.

## Late Payment and Bounce Charges

A grace period of 3 days applies after the EMI date. After the grace period, a late payment charge of 2% per month is levied on the overdue amount. A bounce charge of Rs 500 applies each time an auto-debit fails because of insufficient funds.

## Prepayment and Foreclosure

Part-prepayment and full foreclosure are allowed only after 6 EMIs have been paid. Part-prepayment is limited to 25% of the outstanding principal once per financial year and carries no charge. Foreclosure carries a charge of 4% of the outstanding principal. Individual borrowers on a floating interest rate pay no foreclosure charge.

## Supplier Invoice Financing Repayment

Supplier invoice financing is repaid as a single bullet payment of the financed amount plus markup on the due date. Early repayment is allowed at any time, and the markup is then charged only for the days actually used, subject to a minimum of 15 days.
