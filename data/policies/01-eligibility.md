# Loan Eligibility Policy

Applies to all retail and business loan products offered by Northwind Credit.

## Age Requirements

Applicants must be at least 21 years old at the time of application. The applicant's age at loan maturity must not exceed 60 years for salaried applicants and 65 years for self-employed applicants.

## Income Requirements

Salaried applicants must have a minimum net monthly income of Rs 25,000. Self-employed applicants must show a minimum average monthly income of Rs 40,000, computed from the last two years of income tax returns.

## Credit Score

The minimum credit bureau score is 700. Applicants with a score between 650 and 699 may be approved only with a co-applicant whose score is 720 or higher. Applications with a score below 650 are declined automatically. Applicants with no credit history ("new to credit") are eligible only for loans up to Rs 1,00,000.

## Employment and Business Stability

Salaried applicants must have at least 1 year of total work experience and at least 6 months with their current employer. Self-employed applicants and businesses must have a business vintage of at least 3 years.

## Debt-to-Income Limit

The fixed obligation to income ratio (FOIR) including the proposed EMI must not exceed 50% of net monthly income. For applicants earning more than Rs 1,00,000 per month, the FOIR limit is relaxed to 60%.
