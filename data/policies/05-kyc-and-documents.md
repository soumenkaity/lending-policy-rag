# KYC and Documentation Policy

## Identity Verification

A PAN card is mandatory for every applicant and co-applicant. Identity and address are verified through Aadhaar-based eKYC or, if the applicant prefers, through an Officially Valid Document (passport, voter ID or driving licence). Video KYC is accepted as a substitute for in-person verification.

## Income Documents for Salaried Applicants

Salaried applicants must submit salary slips for the last 3 months and bank statements for the last 6 months showing salary credits.

## Income Documents for Self-Employed Applicants and Businesses

Self-employed applicants must submit income tax returns for the last 2 years and bank statements for the last 12 months. Businesses must additionally submit GST returns for the last 12 months and a certificate of registration or incorporation.

## Supplier Onboarding

Before a supplier can receive invoice financing, the supplier must provide its GSTIN, PAN, and a cancelled cheque or bank letter, and its bank account must pass the penny-drop test.

## Periodic Re-KYC

Customers are re-verified periodically based on their risk category: every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers, and every 10 years for low-risk customers.
