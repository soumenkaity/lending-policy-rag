# Disbursement Policy

## Pre-Disbursement Checks

Funds are disbursed only after the loan agreement has been e-signed, all KYC checks have passed, and the beneficiary bank account has been verified through a penny-drop test. A penny-drop test deposits Rs 1 into the account and confirms that the account holder name matches the borrower or the approved supplier.

## Disbursement Timelines

Loans are disbursed within 2 business days after the loan agreement is e-signed. Requests completed before the 3:00 PM IST cut-off are processed the same business day. Requests completed after the cut-off are processed on the next business day.

## Beneficiary Rules

Personal loans and business term loans are disbursed only to the borrower's own verified bank account. Supplier invoice financing is disbursed directly to the approved supplier's verified bank account and never to the merchant.

## Approval Limits

Disbursements above Rs 25,00,000 require dual approval from two authorised credit officers before release.

## Failed Disbursements

If a disbursement fails, for example because of an invalid account or a bank outage, the system retries automatically up to 3 times at 2-hour intervals. After 3 failed attempts the disbursement is moved to manual review by the operations team, and the borrower is notified by SMS and email.
